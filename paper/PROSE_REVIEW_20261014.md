# Whole-manuscript prose review

The introduction now follows one argument: clinically distinct risks need a
broader assessment; isolated models fragment supervision; shared predictive
patterns motivate pooling; prior learning/transfer work supplies the precedent;
the factorial, tabular and nation-scale/DAPT comparisons resolve attribution.
Repeated qualifications are consolidated, and the clinical motivation remains
at the opening rather than interrupting the argument repeatedly.

The abstract is hypothesis-centred and no longer claims improvement before
results. The Discussion develops clinical, learning and infrastructure
implications without claiming deployment benefit, automatic rare-cell superiority
or absence of information from a weak prediction. Results retain figure-first
layout and explicit empirical fields rather than invented conclusions.

Methods were checked for consistency: local temporal encoding is scaled
Time2Vec; supervised epochs are a planned budget; the fitted-seed roster and
seed-summary estimand require completed-run confirmation. Nation-scale cutoff
provenance remains an explicit audit item. The scarcity spline is regularised,
not guaranteed to be flat by the data; priors and practical margins remain
scientific locks. Source anchoring, primary censoring estimand, population
coverage, exact external architecture and completed training still need final
artifact/protocol reconciliation.

This is an improved working paper, not final Results prose. Main claims must
be rewritten around the actual estimates, and the supplement/input provenance
must be reconciled before submission. Existing source checks are preserved in
INTRODUCTION_CITATION_AUDIT.md; this edit introduces no new literature claims.

## Interpretation

Borrowed updates fit the study's question about adaptive sharing, but outcome-indexed components cease to be trained exclusively on their named outcome. Routing maps can describe component use; paired source removal is needed to assess contribution. A larger learned residual does not imply improved predictions. If outcome borrowing adds little after its optimization is verified, that is a result to report rather than a reason to prolong model development.

Source-bank comparisons support claims about the incremental value of disease/outcome components on top of common multi-outcome supervision and query context. They do not by themselves establish that selective off-home relationship learning causes an improvement over extra disease-conditioned capacity or fixed sharing. A stronger mechanistic claim requires a specified home-only/fixed-routing comparator, with trained and post-hoc controls distinguished. The scientific goal is to test both banks and their combination, not to require the combined version to win.

## 1. Decision: which local pretraining checkpoint

**Use `best.ckpt` from the completed 20-epoch 256-wide run as the local encoder.
Keep `epoch_010.ckpt` only as a recorded check, not as a second arm.**

Why:

- The learning rate is constant after a short warmup (no cosine decay), so
  `epoch_010.ckpt` is a genuine 10-epoch model and `best.ckpt` is simply the
  validation-best point of a longer run of the same model. Continuing to 20
  epochs can only help the local baseline; `best.ckpt` picks whichever epoch
  validated best.
- The local encoder is the baseline the national encoder must beat. Giving it
  the full budget makes "national beats local" a conservative claim; stopping
  it at 10 to mirror PHAIR would weaken the baseline.
- Matching *epochs* with PHAIR does not match *training*: PHAIR's 10 epochs
  run over the whole Danish population, many times more patients and updates
  than 20 epochs over our cohort. The paper therefore reports both encoders'
  budgets (steps, tokens, from the sidecars) and frames the comparison as
  "encoders as delivered", which the manuscript already does.
- Record from the sidecars and logs: validation loss at epoch 10 and at the
  selected epoch, the selected epoch, steps and tokens. If epoch 10 and the
  selected epoch are close, say so in the Supplementary Methods.

**Adaptation (DAPT) of the PHAIR encoder: 20 epochs** (`OPERA_PAPER_MIN_DAPT_EPOCHS=20`,
the default). The adapted national encoder then sees our records exactly as
many times as the local encoder did, so *adapted national vs local* differs
only in the starting point (national pretraining vs none) — the cleanest
reading of that contrast. A 10-epoch DAPT is possible if compute is short (the
workflow now handles it), but then adapted national vs local also differs in
local exposure.

**The running local job.** It was started before today's fixes, so when it
finishes its sidecar will record 21 completed epochs (an off-by-one fixed
today in `bonsai/run/pretrain.py`; Lightning's epoch counter already equals the
completed count after `fit`). The gate (≥ 20) still passes and nothing needs
rerunning; report 20 epochs, confirmed from `metrics.csv`. Do not point
`OPERA_PAPER_LOCAL_CKPT` at a checkpoint before the run has written
`checkpoint_metadata.json` — the gate blocks until the run has finished.

Decide the checkpoint **before `prepare`**: its path is frozen into the bundle.


```bash

scripts/run_paper_experiments.sh dapt --execute              # 20 epochs
```

write the decisions JSON (`source_mode`, then `rank`), then the router lock;
then `prepare --stage 2 --include-external --execute` and let the workers run
`final_sources` and `external`.

(below), then `evaluate --stage 2 --split held_out`, `inventory --stage 2`,
`analyse`, `prepare-bayes`, `bayes --prior-only`, `bayes`, `init-figures`,
`plot-tables`, `embedding-tables`, `figures --validate-render --execute`,

## Controls added on 15 October

- **Disease-pooled XGBoost** (model `XP`): one XGBoost per outcome and horizon
  across all diseases (disease not given as a feature, as for the neural
  pooled models); runs inside the normal `tabular`
  step (69 outcomes × 6 horizons extra fits; `--no-pooled` skips them). The
  inventory scores it in each disease's cell. Contrasts: `T6` joint vs pooled
  XGBoost, `T7` pooled vs per-disease XGBoost, `T8` disease-pooled neural vs
  pooled XGBoost.
- **No-pretraining control** (model `J_RI`): one joint model from a randomly
  initialised encoder of the same architecture, in the stage-1 bundle as phase
  `pretraining_control`; workers run it after J, D and O. Contrast `P0`: local
  pretraining vs none. It needs the local checkpoint only for architecture and
  vocabulary, so it waits for the same completion gate.
- Both are in `final_contrasts_20261014.yaml` and the plot tables.

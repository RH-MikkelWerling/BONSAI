# Lancet Digital Health formatting audit

Source: user-supplied `~/Downloads/tldh-info-for-authors.pdf`, dated August 2026. Page numbers below refer to PDF pages. This supersedes provisional advice based on inaccessible web copies. No manuscript changes were made during this audit.

## Substantive submission differences

- **Article length (p 5):** up to 3,500 words for manuscript text, 30 references. The 4,500-word allowance is for randomised trials. We need a proper main-text-only count after macro expansion and removal of placeholders. The current combined reading bibliography contains 83 entries, including supplementary citations, so it is not a valid main-only reference count.
- **Summary (p 5):** no more than 300 words, five paragraphs headed Background, Methods, Findings, Interpretation and Funding. Current abstract is unstructured and contains drafting instructions.
- **Research in context (p 6):** current panel has the right three sections but needs a completed, documented search and supported findings. No references in this panel or summary.
- **References (p 8):** sequential Vancouver superscripts after punctuation. Current natbib bracketed numbers/unsrtnat are not the requested final presentation. Supplement references must be separately numbered (p 9).
- **Supplement (pp 8–9):** one PDF, table of contents and numbered pages; text 10-point Times New Roman single-spaced, main heading 12-point bold, other headings 10-point bold; table text 8-point. Current supplement is appended to the reading article and embeds a separately built definitions document with its own formatting. Prepare a standalone coherent appendix.
- **Title page (p 7):** author names, one preferred degree each, full affiliations/addresses; separately identified corresponding author with address, email and telephone. These details are not complete in the draft.
- **Funding role (p 4):** belongs at the end of Methods, currently a standalone section near the end.
- **Data sharing (p 6):** specify what data/documents, when, where, access criteria and restrictions. The current legal-access statement needs concrete contact/access and protocol/SAP details.
- **Reporting (p 5):** observational cohort reports should follow STROBE and include their protocols. TRIPOD+AI remains relevant in addition, rather than replacing this explicit requirement.
- **Contributors (p 1):** identify more than one author who directly accessed and verified underlying participant-level data. Confirm actual roles; do not invent them.
- **AI assistance (p 3):** research Articles can use AI with human oversight and accountability, with a disclosure statement. The stricter grammar/spelling-only policy is for Comments and Correspondence, not this original research Article.

## Presentation differences

- Main text font/size: the guide explicitly says not to worry about these (p 7). Our reading fonts are not themselves a submission problem.
- Use midline decimal points, an Oxford comma, numbers one to ten in words except units/figures/tables, and no bold emphasis in body text (p 7).
- Retain page numbers; avoid automated headers/footers in the submission version (p 7). Current reading-copy watermarks are review aids.
- Numbers must accompany percentages. Descriptive means use SDs and medians use IQRs (p 9). This does not mean paired mean model differences should lose their confidence intervals or become medians.
- Kaplan–Meier displays require numbers at risk, with censored counts encouraged (p 5).
- Editable vector PDF and SVG are explicitly accepted for illustrations (p 8), as are several other formats. Word or PowerPoint is requested for study-flow diagrams. Do not flatten plot text to outlines or supply only raster plots.

## Corrections to provisional advice

The supplied guide does **not** state a blanket five-display-item cap or ban Results subheadings. Do not treat either as a verified rule. It also does not require all vector figures to be converted to Word. The concern about excessive display items remains editorial rather than an explicit cap from this PDF.

Priority: shorten main text, select 30 main references and separate appendix references, structure summary, build a standalone supplement, then prepare title-page/declarations and final typography. Retain the current reading PDF for iteration.

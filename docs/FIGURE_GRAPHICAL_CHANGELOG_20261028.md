## Clinical concepts: what “2/3” meant

That title counted direction checks with an estimable effect, not the number of
clinical concepts the model understood. It was an unhelpful success score, and
silently hid unmapped concepts. It has been removed. A boxed expectation is a
plausibility check, not a causal effect or an independent validation metric.

The proposed mappings are in `ATTRIBUTION_CONCEPT_MAPPING_REVIEW_20261028.csv`.
Public hospital assay lists support the proposed NPU identities for LDH,
albumin, haemoglobin, neutrophils, platelets, creatinine and CRP, plus additional
ALT, ALP, bilirubin, ferritin, lymphocyte, eosinophil, sodium and potassium
candidates. The draft LDH assignment to NPU19123 could not be verified. It now
proposes the publicly verified LDH code NPU19658, pending vocabulary coverage.
Sources are linked per row. This checks the public assay identity, not whether
the study vocabulary contains it or whether the encoded bins indicate clinical
abnormality. “Upper/lower bins” must not be interpreted as clinical high/low
without the bin boundaries and reference ranges.

Follow-up: the [Danish registry code audit](DANISH_CODE_AUDIT_20261028.md) found
specific positive-result Patobank codes for TP53 mutation/deletion, MYC
translocation and unmutated IGHV. They are now explicit, exact-match proposals
with their negative-result exclusions recorded. Actual vocabulary coverage and
historical pathology validity still need review. Broad morphology codes do not
establish molecular results. The audit also corrected broad diagnosis/drug/
procedure labels and standardised relative-bin wording throughout the prototypes.

The summary supports `--allow-provisional-concepts` for **tuning only**. It
writes visible `mapping_status` and `attribution_concept_coverage.csv`. Held-out
summaries reject provisional mappings. Additional concepts proposed during
this rehearsal are exploratory, even if an older config called its other
checks prespecified. No fresh IG integration is needed merely to relabel or
resummarize completed token exports.

## How to read routing and embeddings

The revised router distributes weight broadly. That is compatible with useful
partial pooling and does not by itself demonstrate selective source choice.
Gate weights are not predictive contributions: adapters differ in their outputs,
and the shared path remains outside the routing mixture. The source-removal
figures address dependence of the fitted predictions on source corrections.
Their near-zero shrunken pair effects are real properties of the supplied
estimator and are not evidence of a display-scale bug. Raw and shrunken views
are both available for audit.

A large effective count calculated after averaging gates can conceal sharply
different patient routes. The current usage panel D deliberately describes the
mean profile. Historical diagnostics indicate substantially broader patient
routing in the revised fit than in the original fit, but they cover one seed.
They cannot establish cross-seed stability or biological specificity.

Embedding galleries colour a **shared patient representation** by each outcome's
final routed risk. The patient's position is held fixed, while the predictions
can vary by outcome because of the readout and routing. Projected arrows are a
separate descriptive view of corrections in that same projection, with one
magnification. They are not causal effects, and a two-dimensional projection
can hide a substantial correction in the full representation.

Calibration colours sample an IPCW kernel estimate of observed minus predicted
risk at each patient position. They are not a binary event residual per patient.
Changing the display from a surface to points does not remove kernel smoothing
or make local calibration a patient-level estimate.

## What cannot yet be called finished

The local scientific evidence is sufficient to write and audit the pooling,
local benchmark, primary/original router and source-removal results. It is not
sufficient to fill the clinical superiority headline, national pretraining,
recent-week removal, full attribution, selected-strategy or computational-cost
claims. The separate snapshot records what is available without filling these
gaps with old results or synthetic plots.

A successful render is not proof of correct spacing. The audit records geometry
candidates, and the gallery permits visual comparison. Final approval still
requires the fresh real clinical, attribution, coordinate and national exports,
followed by the same graphical pass at the intended print size. No test data
were opened, no model was retrained and no patient arrays were downloaded.

A further support correction: the downloaded one-year comparison roster has
1,575 disease–outcome rows with recorded training counts, including 52 actual
zeros. On this roster 43.9% have fewer than 20 training events, 73.3% fewer than
100, and 92.9% fewer than 500. These are roster-specific descriptive quantities.
The pooled estimable-only roster has 675 rows and 5.3% below 20. Neither should
be confused with treating every unrecorded count in a Cartesian grid as zero.
The refreshed full training metadata remains the authoritative final roster.

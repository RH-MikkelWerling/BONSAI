## 4. Figure work (CPU; explicitly exploratory)

`paper/render/xgboost_predictability_validation_prototype.png` and PDF are
rendered from the 87 pooled XGBoost tuning aggregates. They show binary
fixed-horizon AUROC versus validation event support and 17 display-only
clinical endpoint groups,
not a neural comparison or a training-rarity effect. The CSV lacks a horizon
column; the 365-day label comes from the source run provenance and must be
verified before publication. The shared Python style now prefers Arial on
Windows and metric-compatible Liberation Sans on Linux. Review a server render
before locking dimensions because font fallback may differ.

The archived `weightinggroups` neural plot now shows all six horizons. Its
training-event x-axis is not a causal rarity experiment: longer windows have
more events and generally lower CIF-AUC. Within each horizon the negative
rank association remains at 30/60/90/180 days (Spearman rho approximately
-0.39/-0.33/-0.33/-0.34), including after a >=100 training-event filter,
but weakens at 365/730 days (approximately -0.17/-0.17). Across the 84
outcomes with both 30- and 730-day scores, 78 have lower CIF-AUC at 730 days.
These patterns motivate a matched-outcome, fixed-horizon analysis with
uncertainty and endpoint-class adjustment; they do not identify the effect of
adding training cases. Keep this caveat in the figure caption.
